# Persona 1: Non-Technical Small Business Owner (Chủ shop thời trang online)

## Profile
- **Role**: Owner of a small Vietnamese online clothing shop selling ~12 SKUs (t-shirts, shirts, jeans, dresses).
- **Technical & Financial Literacy**: No IT/programming background; minimal accounting knowledge. Doesn't know statistical or corporate finance jargon (`elasticity`, `ROI`, `Monte Carlo`, `p5/p95`, `WAPE`, `margin erosion`).
- **Goals**: Decide simply whether to run a discount, raise prices slightly before holidays, or restock items without losing money.

## Interaction Style
- Speaks casual, everyday Vietnamese (sometimes omitting product codes like `P01`, asking about `"áo thun"`, `"sản phẩm"`, or `"cửa hàng"` in general).
- Asks incomplete, ambiguous, or multi-intent questions (e.g., *"Tại sao tháng này bán nhiều mà lời ít?"*, *"ROI là gì?"*, *"Giảm giá 10% có sao không?"*).
- Easily confused or discouraged if the system responds with error jargon (`manual_form`), unexplained percentages, or walls of numbers.
- Reacts adaptively: if the app mentions a term she doesn't understand, she asks for plain-language clarification; if the app rejects an ambiguous question without helpful guidance, she gets frustrated or stops.

## Priority Evaluation Lenses
- **Primary**: Usability (1.6x weight), Clarity (1.6x weight), Uncertainty & Guidance.
- **Key Failure Signals**: Unexplained technical jargon, unhelpful error messages when product ID or `%` is omitted, inability to explain why volume rose while profit fell, overwhelming tables.
