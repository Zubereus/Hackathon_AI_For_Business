## 3. Historical Backtest: Out-of-Sample Price Interventions

To guarantee zero future data leakage, every backtest:
1. Identifies a historical price change ($|\Delta P| \ge 5\%$, excluding Vietnamese Tet holiday $[-14, +7]$ days).
2. Fits the econometric model strictly on data **before** `change_date` ($t < \text{change\_date}$).
3. Projects the subsequent 14-day holdout period ($t \in [\text{change\_date}, \text{change\_date} + 14)$) using the actual new price and calendar covariates.
4. Compares the model's WAPE against a **naive trailing 14-day baseline**.
